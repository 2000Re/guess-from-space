Guess From Space is a geography quiz you can play in 15 seconds.

Each video starts with a close-up satellite image of a famous place, then zooms out while you choose from three answers. Can you recognize Venice, Mount Fuji or the Palm Jumeirah from space?

🌍 New quizzes every day
🛰️ World Heritage Sites, volcanoes, islands, cities and natural wonders
💬 Comment your guess before the answer, and tell us your score!
